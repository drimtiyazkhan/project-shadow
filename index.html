Workflow
How it works
1

Download Bulk Deal CSV from NSE

Go to NSE website → Market Data → Bulk Deals → select date range → Download CSV. Or use the filtered CSV you already prepare weekly.

2

Upload to the Generator

Open the Generator and drop your CSV file. The tool reads it instantly in your browser — no upload to any server. Previous data is remembered automatically from last time.

3

Select traders and generate

Choose which HFT traders to include — all 12, or individual ones. Click Generate Pine Script. Done in under a second.

4

Paste into TradingView

Click Copy to Clipboard. Open TradingView Pine Editor → paste → Save. Green arrows appear on every stock where HFTs were active.

5

Update weekly in 30 seconds

Each week just upload the new CSV. The tool adds new data to your existing history automatically, regenerates the script, and you paste once more.
