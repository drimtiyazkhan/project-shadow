Our Thesis
Why we track these accounts

Every stock that moves sharply has someone behind the move. In the Indian markets, we've observed a recurring pattern: a specific group of HFT trading accounts shows up, again and again, in the bulk deal data of stocks right before or during major price action. We call the entity coordinating this activity the operator.

01

Frequent, Repeated Footprints

We didn't pick these 12 accounts at random. They were shortlisted because of how often — and how consistently — they appear in bulk deals right around periods of sharp, manipulative-looking price action.

02

Signal, Not Noise

Our belief is that this activity is not incidental — it's a signal that operator interest is present in a stock. When several of these accounts show up on the same stock and date, that's when the signal gets strongest.

03

Trade With the Operator

The goal isn't to predict the operator's next move blindly — it's to avoid being trapped in a retail-facing plot, and instead position alongside where the real activity is concentrated.

This is our trading methodology and interpretation of publicly available NSE bulk deal data — not a factual claim about any individual entity's intent. Bulk deal disclosures are public record; our indicator simply visualizes patterns in that data on your TradingView chart. Always do your own research before acting on any signal.

On The Chart
What the indicator actually looks like
Hover any marker to see the exact date, how many of the 12 tracked accounts were active, and every account name present that day.
Marker size and highlighting scale automatically with how many tracked accounts were active on a given day.
1

Marks every candle with activity

Any day one of the 12 tracked accounts appears in the bulk deal data, the indicator drops a marker directly on that candle — no digging through spreadsheets.

2

Hover for full detail

Move your cursor over any marker and a tooltip pops up with the date, stock, and the full list of tracked account names active that day.

3

Marker size shows conviction

A single account shows a small marker; several tracked accounts on the same day show a visibly larger one — the size itself tells you how significant the day was, at a glance.

4

Vertical highlighting

Candles with tracked activity get a soft vertical highlight down the chart, so clusters of activity are visible even when zoomed out.

5

Saves hours of manual work

No more manually cross-referencing bulk deal CSVs against a chart, day by day, stock by stock — the indicator does it for every symbol automatically.

6

Surfaces historical operator levels

Scroll back through months or years of chart history and instantly see exactly where tracked accounts were active before — a fast way to find historically significant price levels.
